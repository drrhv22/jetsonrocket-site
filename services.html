Services

Everything a rental needs, handled by one accountable person

No departments, no ticket queues. The person who takes on your property is the person who runs it — these are the things we take off your plate.

Property care & oversight

The day-to-day needs of your rental, documented as we go.

  • Tenant communication and issue tracking
  • Regular property checks, with photos when you want them
  • Move-in and move-out coordination, including condition documentation
  • Records kept of transactions and key decisions

Listing & screening support

Finding a tenant worth keeping is most of the job — we support you through it, and the decisions stay yours.

  • Help preparing the listing and scheduling showings
  • Application materials organized for your review — credit, background, and income reports through standard screening services
  • Lease documents sourced for the property's jurisdiction, ready for your signature
  • Move-in walk-through and deposit documentation

Maintenance & vendor management

We arrange repairs and upkeep, and we oversee the people doing the work.

  • Repair requests assessed and scheduled — urgent issues handled first
  • Quotes from vendors we've used before where we have them — carefully vetted new ones where we don't
  • Work verified, in person or with photo documentation, before invoices are paid
  • Preventive upkeep suggested before small problems become large ones

Lease & compliance support

Paperwork done right, in the jurisdiction your property lives in.

  • Lease documents sourced for the property's jurisdiction, with local legal review when needed
  • Notices and renewals handled on time
  • Help navigating local and state rental requirements
  • When situations get difficult, we bring in a local attorney rather than improvising

Owner reporting

You should never wonder what's happening at your property.

  • A monthly statement: income recorded, expenses paid, receipts attached
  • Notes on maintenance completed and anything coming up
  • Year-end totals ready for tax preparation

Tell us about your property. We reply within one to two business days.
