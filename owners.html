For property owners

What you get when we manage your property

We own rental property ourselves, so we built the service we always wanted as owners: direct answers, documented money, and no surprises.

The essentials

A direct line

You talk to the person who manages your property — not a call center, not a rotating account rep.

Documented money

Income and expenses itemized on your monthly statement, with receipts for expenses.

Bilingual handling

Tenants and vendors handled in English or Spanish — useful in California, essential in Michoacán.

A simple flat fee

One monthly fee, quoted after the initial walk-through. You'll know it before you sign anything.

How reporting works

Each month you get a statement by email: income recorded, expenses paid with receipts attached, maintenance completed, and anything coming up that needs a decision from you. At year end, you get totals organized for tax preparation. If something urgent happens mid-month, you hear about it then — not in the report.

Common questions

What does it cost?
A flat monthly fee, quoted after the initial walk-through. The number depends on the property and what it needs — you'll have it in writing before committing to anything.
What kinds of property do you take on?
Single-family homes, warehouses, and agricultural land — we manage each of those in our own portfolio. Something else? Ask — it depends on the property.
Do you work outside your four regions?
Our regions are the SF Bay Area, Southern California, Central Texas, and Michoacán. If your property is near one of them, ask — it depends on the property.
¿Hablan español?
Sí. Service is bilingual English/Spanish in every region, for owners and tenants alike.
